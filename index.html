How it works

Don't think it through.

You will rank eight colours twice. Between the passes, rest your eyes. First choices often carry the story you tell. Second choices often carry the weather you are actually in. Keep what fits. Leave the rest.

About

A snapshot, not a verdict.

In 1947 Max Lüscher proposed that people reveal a present inner weather by ranking colours. Chromora borrows that gesture — eight colours, two passes — and writes its own readings. Official scoring tables are not used.
